Second Chapter About Māra

SN 23.11 About Māra Mārasutta

At Sāvatthī.

Seated to one side, Venerable Rādha said to the Buddha:

“Sir, they speak of ‘Māra’. How is Māra defined?”

“Rādha, form is Māra, feeling is Māra, perception is Māra, choices are Māra, consciousness is Māra.

Seeing this, a learned noble disciple grows disillusioned with form, feeling, perception, choices, and consciousness. Being disillusioned, desire fades away. When desire fades away they’re freed. When they’re freed, they know they’re freed.

They understand: ‘Rebirth is ended, the spiritual journey has been completed, what had to be done has been done, there is nothing further for this place.’”

SN 23.12 Subject to Māra Māradhammasutta

At Sāvatthī.

Seated to one side, Venerable Rādha said to the Buddha:

“Sir, they speak of ‘Māra nature’.1587 What is of Māra nature?”

“Rādha, form is of Māra nature. Feeling, perception, choices, and consciousness are of Māra nature.

Seeing this … They understand: ‘… there is nothing further for this place.’”
