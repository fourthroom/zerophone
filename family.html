PICTURE THIS! – Official Game Rules

The Objective

You are an elite art collector navigating the ruthless world of high-stakes auctions. Your goal is to amass the highest net worth by the time the bank's gallery is empty. Your net worth is the combined total of your cash on hand and the true, hidden value of the authentic artworks in your portfolio.

Game Setup

  1. The Bank: Appoint one player (or your WebRTC cloud umpire) to act as the Banker. The Banker manages the cash, the Artwork deck, and the Value deck.
  2. Starting Capital: The Banker issues $15,000,000 to each player (e.g., one $5M, nine $1M, and two $500k notes).
  3. The Starting Portfolio: Shuffle the Artwork and Value decks separately. The Banker deals one Artwork card face-up to each player, followed by one Value card face-down.
  4. Secret Values: Players secretly slide their Value card underneath their Artwork card. You may look at your own Value cards at any time, but you must keep them hidden from your opponents.

On Your Turn

Players take turns rolling a standard 6-sided die and moving their token clockwise around the 30-space circular track. You must execute the action of the space you land on. Two or more players may occupy the same space.

The Board Spaces

1. Bank Auction (11 Spaces)

The Bank places the top Artwork card from the deck onto the center easel.

  • The Banker opens the bidding. Any player may bid, regardless of whose turn it is.
  • Bids must be in increments of at least $500,000.
  • The highest bidder pays their bid amount to the Bank, takes the Artwork card, and immediately draws the top Value card, sliding it secretly behind their new painting.
  • Note: If no one bids, the Artwork goes to the bottom of the deck.

2. Private Auction (3 Spaces)

You must auction off one of the artworks from your own portfolio to the other players!

  • The player to your immediate left chooses which of your paintings goes on the block.
  • You act as the auctioneer and cannot bid.
  • The highest bidder pays you directly. They take the Artwork card and all Value cards attached to it. The buyer cannot look at the attached Value cards until the transaction is complete.
  • Note: If you own no artwork when you land here, nothing happens and your turn ends.

3. Direct Purchase (7 Spaces: $1.5M, $2.0M, or $2.5M)

You have the exclusive option to buy the top Artwork card directly from the Bank for the price printed on the space.

  • If you choose to buy, pay the Bank, take the Artwork, and draw the top Value card.
  • No other player may bid or interfere.
  • If you decline the purchase, the Artwork remains on top of the deck and your turn ends.

4. Collect $3,000,000 OR a Value Card (5 Spaces)

You are presented with a market choice: liquidity or asset inflation.

  • Option A: Take $3,000,000 in cash from the Bank.
  • Option B: Draw the top Value card from the deck and secretly add it to an Artwork you already own. This allows you to increase a painting's worth by stacking multiple Value cards behind it.
  • Note: If you do not own any artwork, you must take the cash.

5. Inherit a Painting (4 Spaces)

A wealthy benefactor has left you a masterpiece. Take the top Artwork card and the top Value card from the Bank completely free of charge.

The Portfolio Rules (Values & Forgeries)

  • Stacking: A single Artwork can have multiple Value cards stacked behind it. Its total worth is the sum of all its attached cards.
  • Permanence: Once a Value card is assigned to an Artwork, it cannot be moved to a different painting.
  • The Forgery Rule: If any Value card attached to an Artwork is a "Forgery", that painting is definitively proven fake. Its total value drops to $0, regardless of how many other legitimate Value cards are stacked with it.
  • Bluffing: You are highly encouraged to hype up your forgeries to trick opponents into buying them during Private Auctions!

Game End & Victory

The game instantly ends the moment the final Artwork card is acquired from the Bank.

At this point, all players reveal their Value cards.

  1. Discard the value of any painting containing a Forgery card.
  2. Sum the valid Value cards in your portfolio.
  3. Add your remaining cash on hand.

The player with the highest total Net Worth is the ultimate collector and wins the game!
